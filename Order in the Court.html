Order in the Court

You were sworn in this morning. The courtroom has folding chairs and a flickering light. The cases are messier than the room. Climb from District Court to the Supreme Court, one ruling at a time.

Judge

This browser has no built-in voice, so text-to-speech is off.

How a case works

  1. Open the case file. Read the evidence. Nobody tells you the charge.
  2. Bang the gavel. The clock on the wall starts ticking. Hear both sides, or one person and a police report.
  3. Dig. 3 questions, a witness, a clue mini-game. Tap a speech bubble to flag a lie.
  4. Rule. Crime or No Crime. For a crime, find the law in the law book and pick a fair sentence.

Day 1 is a guided orientation. After that, envelopes will land on your desk, and nobody tells you how close the press is.
